## Opening a card

Choose a card's title (or anywhere on its preview) to open the tool. The tool fills the page inside a preview frame, with a small floating control bar for the page around it:

- The tool's title opens an info card about the exhibit.
- The star button returns to **Starred**.
- The pencil opens **Edit** (owners only).
- **Share** opens sharing (owners only).
- **More** holds extra actions, including opening outside the preview (see below).

Cards for tools shared with you open the same way but without the owner's editing controls.

## Using the tool normally

Just use it: click, type, and change things as the tool invites you to. Anything the tool saves — your entries, settings, progress — is kept for you. If a tool misbehaves or looks stuck, reloading the page gives it a fresh start; anything you had not yet saved in the tool may be lost.

Occasionally a banner appears over the tool:

- If the preview cannot provide something the tool needs, a warning explains it and offers **Open in new tab**.
- If something changed elsewhere while the page was open, a notice invites you to **Reload artifact** to use the latest saved data.

## What follows you between devices

Sign in with the same account on another device, open the same card, and your saved entries and settings are there — what you put into a tool travels with your account.

One exception: things a tool keeps only for the current tab never leave that tab. Close the tab and they are gone on every device.

## When a tool asks to connect or download

The first time a tool wants something beyond showing itself, Exhibit asks you before allowing it. Each prompt names the tool and offers an allow and a block choice; allowing remembers your answer for that tool, and you can change it later under **Edit → Permissions**.

- **Allow network access?** — the tool tried to contact a named site and was blocked. Choose **Allow site** to permit it from now on, **Block once** to refuse just this time, or **Always block site** to refuse it permanently.
- **Allow downloads?** — the tool wants to save a named file to your device. Choose **Allow downloads** or **Block**.
- **Allow clipboard access?** — the tool wants to use your clipboard. Choose **Allow clipboard** or **Block**.
- **Allow opening links?** — you are opening a link to a named site. Choose **Allow links** or **Block**.
- **Allow device access?** — the tool wants your camera or microphone. Choosing **Allow and open** opens the tool in a new tab to use the device; your browser will also ask for permission.
- **Allow location access?** — the tool wants your location. Choosing **Allow and open** opens the tool in a new tab, where your browser will also ask.

If you are viewing a tool someone shared with you, only its owner can allow new sites — you will see an explanation naming the blocked site instead of a prompt.

## Full view

Some tools work better outside the preview frame — for downloads, outside links, or device features. Choose **Open in new tab** (from the More menu, or from a banner when one is offered).

The first time, a confirmation titled **Open in a new tab?** explains that the tool will run as a regular web page under your browser's normal controls. Tick **Do not ask again for this artifact** to skip this confirmation next time, then choose **Open in new tab** — or **Cancel** to stay in the preview.
