Use your gallery's four spaces to find tools, then use stars, tags, and collections to keep them easy to reach.

## The four spaces

- **Starred** is your gallery home, where you find the tools you've starred.
- **Search** lets you search your full library.
- **Shared** lists tools you've shared and tools shared with you, with a count.
- **Activity** shows recent changes and running agents.

## Search

1. Go to **Search**.
2. Type in the **Search** box and press Enter. Press `/` to jump to the box.
3. To clear the box, select the × button labeled **Clear search**.

Search checks titles, words shown on the page, and tag names. If you see "No artifacts match your search.", try another title, keyword, or tag.

## Tags and collections

- **Collections** are named groups of tools.
- **Tags** are labels with colored dots that you attach to tools.

To narrow your results:

1. Choose **Filter** to open the **Filters** panel. On wide screens, you can use the filter list beside the results.
2. Pick a tag or collection. Each active filter appears as a chip above the results.
3. Remove a chip to widen your search, or choose **Clear all** to remove every filter. Choose **Done** to close the panel.

To label a tool, open its edit page and find **Tags**. Adding or removing a tag saves immediately.

## Starring

Star a tool to keep it on your **Starred** home page.

1. Open the card's action menu using the three-dot **Actions** button.
2. Choose **Star**. A star appears on the card.
3. To remove the star, open the same menu and choose **Unstar**.

If you haven't starred anything, you'll see "No starred artifacts yet" and directions to the action menu and the **Search** page.

## The Activity page

- **Running agents** lists what's building now. Choose **Refresh** to update the list. If nothing is running, you'll see "No agents are running right now."
- **Recent changes** lists saved versions and agent changes. Select an entry to view the change. If there haven't been any changes, the list says so.

## The Shared space

In **Shared**, you'll find tools you've shared and tools others have shared with you. When someone shares a tool with you, its card shows the owner's name. You can open the tool in view-only mode. The editing controls belong to the owner.
