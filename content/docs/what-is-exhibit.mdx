Exhibit is a library for small interactive tools — calculators, trackers, planners, little helpers — that you open and use right in your browser.

## The basic ideas

You'll run into these everywhere in the app:

- **Exhibits (your tools).** Each saved tool is one page you can open and use. Think of them like apps in your own little app store.
- **The gallery (your library).** The home screen that lists all your tools as cards. This is where you start.
- **Cards.** One card per tool. Each card shows a small live preview of the tool, its title, and a menu of things you can do with it.
- **Widgets (the little preview on a card).** The small live preview at the top of a card. It shows you what's inside without opening the whole thing.
- **Versions (history you can restore).** When a tool changes, Exhibit keeps the older copies. If something breaks or you preferred the old one, you can go back to it.
- **Sharing (links vs named people).** You can share a tool two ways: with a link anyone can open, or with specific signed-in people. Either way, you can stop sharing whenever you like.
