Exhibit is your library of interactive tools. You can open calculators, trackers, planners, and other small tools right in your browser.

## The basic ideas

- **Exhibits.** Each tool you save is an exhibit, a page you can open and use.
- **Gallery.** Your gallery is where you start. It shows your tools as cards.
- **Cards.** Each tool has a card with a title, a live preview, and an action menu.
- **Widgets.** The live preview at the top of a card is its widget. It lets you see what's inside before opening the tool.
- **Versions.** Exhibit keeps older versions as you change a tool. You can restore one if something breaks or you prefer the way it worked before.
- **Sharing.** You can share with anyone who has the link or with specific signed-in people. You can stop sharing at any time.
