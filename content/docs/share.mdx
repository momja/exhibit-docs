Sharing lives on the artifact's page. Open the artifact, choose the **Share** button in the floating controls, and the **Share artifact** dialog opens. Choose **Done** to close it when you finish.

## Share with a link

1. In the **Share artifact** dialog, find the **Public link** section.
2. Turn on **Anyone with the link can open this artifact**.
3. A **Public link** field appears with the URL. Use its copy button to copy it, then send it to whoever you like.
4. Anyone with the link can open the artifact and see your saved data. They cannot change it — in either data mode, visitors can view your saved data but cannot change it.

To replace a link that got out:

1. Open the **Share artifact** dialog again.
2. Choose **Replace link**. The current link stops working immediately, and a new one appears. Copy the new one.

To stop link sharing, turn **Anyone with the link can open this artifact** off. The URL dies at once.

The **Embed widget** section (under the public link) holds a read-only card widget showing the artifact's saved data — a snippet you can paste elsewhere. Replacing the link replaces this snippet too.

## Share with named people

1. In the **Share artifact** dialog, find the **People** section.
2. Under **Add people**, type existing Exhibit usernames or emails, separated by commas, into the **Username or email** field.
3. Choose **Give access**.

Each person you add appears in the **People with access** list. What they get: they open the artifact as themselves and see your sharing setup; they never get access to your connected accounts (sharing can expose saved results from connected accounts, but never grants account access).

To remove someone, choose **Revoke** beside their name. If nobody is shared with yet, the list reads **Not shared with anyone yet.**

## Your data vs their data

The **Saved data for people you add** section decides whose saved data named people use:

- **Separate data** — Each person uses their own saved data. Changes affect only them.
- **Shared data** — People you add can view and update your saved data. Changes affect everyone with access.

Pick the one that matches what you want, then review your saved data before sharing. Opening **What happens when I switch?** explains the switch both ways: going separate returns everyone to their own previously saved data (or empty, if they have none), while going shared puts people you add onto your saved data and keeps their separate data aside for later.

Link visitors always see your saved data and cannot change it, whichever option you pick.

## What recipients see

A person who opens your share sees the artifact running with the data your settings allow. They cannot edit it, change its sites or permissions, or re-share it — those controls stay yours. If the tool needs a site you never allowed, they see a note naming it and asking them to have you review the sites list.

## Stop sharing

1. Open the **Share artifact** dialog.
2. Turn the public-link toggle off to kill the link.
3. Choose **Revoke** beside each person to remove them.

Once both are done, nobody outside your account can reach the artifact.
