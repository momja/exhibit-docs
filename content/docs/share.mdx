Open the tool you want to share and choose **Share** in the floating toolbar. When you're finished in **Share artifact**, choose **Done**.

## Share with a link

1. In **Share artifact**, find **Public link**.
2. Turn on **Anyone with the link can open this artifact**.
3. When the **Public link** field appears, use its copy button to copy the URL. Send it to the people you want to share with.
4. Anyone with the link can open the tool and view your saved data. Link visitors have read-only access to that data in both data modes.

To replace a link you've already shared:

1. Open **Share artifact** again.
2. Choose **Replace link**. The old link stops working immediately. Copy the new one.

To stop link sharing, turn off **Anyone with the link can open this artifact**. The link stops working immediately.

Below the public link, **Embed widget** gives you a snippet you can paste elsewhere to show a read-only card widget with your saved data. Replacing the link replaces the snippet too.

## Share with named people

1. In **Share artifact**, find **People**.
2. Under **Add people**, enter existing Exhibit usernames or email addresses in **Username or email**. Separate multiple entries with commas.
3. Choose **Give access**.

Each person you add appears in **People with access**. They open the tool using their own account and see the sharing setup you chose. Sharing can show saved results from your connected accounts. Access to those accounts stays with you.

To remove someone, choose **Revoke** beside their name. If the list is empty, it reads **Not shared with anyone yet.**

## Your data vs their data

Use **Saved data for people you add** to choose whose saved data named people use.

- **Separate data.** Each person uses their own saved data. Their changes affect only them.
- **Shared data.** People you add can view and update your saved data. Changes affect everyone with access.

Choose a mode and review your saved data before sharing. Select **What happens when I switch?** to see what changes.

- Switching to **Separate data** returns each person to their own saved data. If they have none, they start with empty data.
- Switching to **Shared data** gives people you add your saved data and keeps their separate data for later.

With either setting, link visitors can view your saved data in read-only mode.

## What recipients see

When someone opens your tool, it runs with the data your sharing settings allow. You keep control over editing, sites, permissions, and sharing. If the tool needs a site you haven't allowed, recipients see a message that names the site and asks them to have you review the sites list.

## Stop sharing

1. Open **Share artifact**.
2. Turn off the public link.
3. Choose **Revoke** beside each person to remove their access.

Once the link is off and you've removed everyone, access is limited to your account.
