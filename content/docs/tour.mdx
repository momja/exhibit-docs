Here's a walk through the parts of Exhibit you'll use every day.

## Signing in

If your Exhibit asks you to sign in, you'll see a page that says **Sign in to your Gallery.** Type your **Username** and **Password**, then press the **Sign in** button. Some setups also offer a **Sign in with SSO** button as a second option.

Once you're in, you land on your gallery.

## Spaces: Starred, Search, Shared, Activity

Down the side of the gallery is a list of spaces — different views of your library:

- **Starred** — your home page. It shows the tools you've starred, so your favorites are one click away.
- **Search** — the full searchable library with filters.
- **Shared** — tools shared with you and tools you've shared, with a count badge.
- **Activity** — what's been happening: running helpers under **Running agents** (with a **Refresh** button) and a **Recent changes** list below.

On a phone, the spaces become tabs across the top instead of a side list.

## Finding things: search and filters

In Search you'll see a search box labeled **Search artifacts** (with a `/` shortcut hint). Type a title, keyword, or tag and the grid updates to match. If nothing matches, the page says so and suggests trying another word.

There's also a **Filter** button that opens a **Filters** panel where you can narrow by category. Press **Done** to close it. Active filters appear as little chips above the results — click one to remove it, or **Clear all** to drop them together.

## What a card shows

Each card in the grid is one tool. From top to bottom:

- **Preview** — the small live widget showing the tool itself.
- **Title** — click it to open the full tool.
- **Star mark** — a star appears on tools you've starred.
- **Permission and sharing marks** — small icons when a tool has extra permissions (like network or camera access) or is shared. Fully private tools with no special permissions show nothing here.
- **Actions menu** (the three-dots button) — opens a menu with **Star** (or **Unstar**), **Edit**, and **Duplicate**.

Clicking anywhere else on the card opens the tool.

## Inside an open tool

Opening a tool shows it large, with a small floating toolbar: a **Starred** button back to home, the tool's name (**About this artifact**), an **Edit artifact** pencil, a **Share** button, and a **More actions** menu with choices like **Open in new tab**, **Edit**, **Edit with agent**, **Download HTML**, and **Hide controls**.

Sometimes a tool asks for permission the first time it needs something — for example **Allow downloads?** with **Allow downloads** and **Block** buttons, or similar prompts for the clipboard, links, camera, or location. Allowing remembers your choice; blocking keeps things as they are. You can change permissions later under Edit.

## Try it yourself: a five-minute tour

1. **Sign in.** Open the address you were given, enter your username and password, and press **Sign in**.
2. **Look at your spaces.** Click **Starred**, **Search**, **Shared**, and **Activity** in turn and see what each one lists.
3. **Open a card.** Click a card's title to open the tool, then use the **Starred** button in the toolbar to go back home.
4. **Star it.** Open the card's Actions menu (three dots) and choose **Star**. It now appears under **Starred**.
5. **Find it again.** Go to **Search**, type part of its title, and watch it come up.
