Take a quick look around your gallery, then open a tool and practice finding it again.

## Signing in

If you're asked to sign in, you'll see **Sign in to your Gallery.** Enter your **Username** and **Password**, then choose **Sign in**. If you see **Sign in with SSO**, you can use that option too.

After signing in, you'll land in your gallery.

## Spaces: Starred, Search, Shared, Activity

Use the spaces along the side of your gallery to switch between views.

- **Starred** is your home page. It shows the tools you've starred.
- **Search** lets you search and filter your full library.
- **Shared** shows tools you've shared and tools shared with you, along with a count.
- **Activity** shows **Running agents**, with a **Refresh** button, and a **Recent changes** list below.

On a phone, you'll see the spaces as tabs across the top.

## Finding things: search and filters

In **Search**, type a title, keyword, or tag into **Search artifacts**. The results update to match. You can jump to the search box with `/`. If the page says there are no matches, try another word.

Choose **Filter** to open the **Filters** panel and narrow your results by category. Choose **Done** to close it. Each active filter appears as a chip above the results. Select a chip to remove that filter or choose **Clear all** to remove them together.

## What a card shows

Each card represents one tool.

- **Preview.** See the tool in the live widget at the top of the card.
- **Title.** Select it to open the full tool.
- **Star mark.** Look for a star on any tool you've starred.
- **Permission and sharing marks.** Look for icons if the tool is shared or has extra permissions, such as network or camera access. This area is empty for private tools with no special permissions.
- **Action menu.** Use the three-dot **Actions** button to choose **Star** (or **Unstar**), **Edit**, or **Duplicate**.

Clicking elsewhere on the card also opens the tool.

## Inside an open tool

When you open a tool, the floating toolbar gives you a few shortcuts.

- Choose **Starred** to go back home.
- Select the tool's name (**About this artifact**) to see its details.
- Use the **Edit artifact** pencil or the **Share** button.
- Open **More actions** for choices like **Open in new tab**, **Edit**, **Edit with agent**, **Download HTML**, and **Hide controls**.

Some tools ask permission the first time they need a feature. For example, **Allow downloads?** offers **Allow downloads** and **Block**. You may also see prompts for the clipboard, links, camera, or location. Exhibit remembers what you allow. If you block a request, the tool continues without that permission. You can change permissions later under **Edit**.

## Try it yourself: a five-minute tour

1. Open the address you were given. Enter your username and password, then choose **Sign in**.
2. Visit **Starred**, **Search**, **Shared**, and **Activity** to see what each space shows.
3. Select a card's title to open the tool. Choose **Starred** in the toolbar to return home.
4. Open the card's action menu (three dots) and choose **Star**. The card now appears under **Starred**.
5. Go to **Search** and type part of the tool's title to find it again.

When you're ready, add a tool of your own or share one with someone else.
